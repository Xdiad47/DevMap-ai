INDIA'S OFFICIAL BOUNDARY (not included on purpose)

The map draws no country or state borders itself. The base map tiles are third-party and their depiction of
India's external boundary (Jammu & Kashmir, Ladakh, Arunachal Pradesh) may not match Survey of India's official map.

Before public launch, either:
  (a) use a tile provider that renders India's boundary per Indian law (e.g. a provider with an India "worldview"), or
  (b) put the official outline here as `india-official-boundary.geojson` (WGS84 GeoJSON, from Survey of India or
      another source your lawyer approves). The map draws it automatically on top of the tiles if the file exists.

Either way, have counsel review the final map. See README "Map & India's borders".
